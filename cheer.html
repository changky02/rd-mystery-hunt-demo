Concept · 60 seconds

Make spectatorship visible. Make support portable.

Friends and family compose a card — photo, message, mile — in two minutes from anywhere. The card lives in three places.

On the runner's phone as a wallet pass push when they approach the cheer zone. On the announcer's tablet — read aloud at the cheer zone with sponsor mention. On supporter phones already at the zone, held up as a crowd display. In the post-race reel as a shareable memory artifact.

No screens. No new hardware. No timing-co dependency.

Why this is different

Not a runner-tracking app. A supporter-first product.

RaceJoy is text-only, sponsor-neutral, and runner-installed. Cheer Card is photo-driven, sponsor-funded, and supporter-installed. Different audience, different revenue, different brand impressions.
