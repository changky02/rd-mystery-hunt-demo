The Notebook

Mission Cafe, behind the wall · 2023

In the spring of 1927, Sara Albright was the most celebrated young runner in Santa Barbara County. Nineteen years old, daughter of a Mission Canyon orchard family, she held the unofficial women's record for what locals called the "maritime mile" — a stretch of coastline she ran daily before sunrise.

Three weeks before her wedding to a railroad heir, she disappeared.

Her notebook was found ninety-six years later, in 2023, behind the wall of the Mission Cafe during a renovation. Inside: training routes, weather notes, and a single underlined sentence on the final page.

"Tomorrow I run the perfect mile. The one I've been searching for since spring. If I'm right, it will change everything."

Sara was never seen again. The route she ran that morning was never identified.
